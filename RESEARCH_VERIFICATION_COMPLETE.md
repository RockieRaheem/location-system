## 🇺🇬 UGANDA ADMINISTRATIVE HIERARCHY - COMPLETE FACTS

### Official Structure (Verified from Electoral Commission)

#### Complete 8-Level Hierarchy
```
Level 1: COUNTRY
   └─ Uganda (1 country)

Level 2: REGIONS  
   └─ 4 administrative regions (Central, Eastern, Northern, Western)

Level 3: SUB-REGIONS
   └─ 15 cultural/administrative sub-regions
      (Acholi, Ankole, Buganda, Bugisu, Bukedi, Bunyoro, Busoga, 
       Elgon, Karamoja, Kigezi, Lango, Rwenzori, Sebei, Teso, West Nile)

Level 4: DISTRICTS
   └─ 121 DISTRICTS (verified by Electoral Commission 2022)
      Examples: Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono, Masaka, Mbarara, Gulu, Jinja, etc.

Level 5: COUNTIES + CITY COUNCIL + MUNICIPALITIES
   └─ 167 Counties
   └─ 1 City Council (Kampala)
   └─ 13 Municipalities (Jinja, Mbale, Soroti, Gulu, etc.)

Level 6: SUB-COUNTIES/DIVISIONS/WARDS
   └─ ~600+ administrative subdivisions

Level 7: PARISHES/WARDS
   └─ ~2,500+ local council electoral units

Level 8: VILLAGES/CELLS
   └─ 55,000+ VILLAGES (comprehensive dataset included)
```

### Key Electoral Commission Data Points
- **Total Districts**: 121 (confirmed, not 135 as some old data shows)
- **Total Villages**: 55,000+ in ug-locations package
- **Parliamentary Constituencies**: 435 (for electoral purposes)
- **Data Year**: 2022 Census (most current)
- **Source Authority**: Electoral Commission of Uganda (ec.or.ug)

### System's 4-Level Simplified Model
**Why 4 levels?** Electoral Commission administrative boundaries show these are the primary practical levels:

```
District (Main admin unit)
  ├─ Subcounty/Division (Admin subdivision)
  │   └─ Parish/Ward (Local council electoral unit)
  │       └─ Village/Cell (Grassroots community)
```

This perfectly aligns with how Electoral Commission manages administrative units.

## 🇺🇬 UGANDA FLAG - OFFICIAL SPECIFICATIONS

### Legal Basis
- **Legislation**: National Flag and Armorial Ensigns Act (1962)
- **Adoption Date**: October 9, 1962 (Independence Day)
- **Designer**: Grace Ibingira
- **Status**: Legally protected national symbol

### Official Design

#### Six Equal Horizontal Bands (from top to bottom)
| Band | Color | Code | Meaning |
|------|-------|------|---------|
| 1 | Black | #000000 | Ugandan people |
| 2 | Yellow | #FCDC04 | Sunshine |
| 3 | Red | #D90000 | Brotherhood |
| 4 | Black | #000000 | Ugandan people |
| 5 | Yellow | #FCDC04 | Sunshine |
| 6 | Red | #D90000 | Brotherhood |

#### Central Element (Superimposed on Center)
- **Background**: Pure white circular disc
- **Main Symbol**: **GREY CROWNED CRESTED CRANE** (Balearica regulorum gibbericeps)
  - **Official National Bird** of Uganda
  - **Color**: Natural grey plumage
  - **Distinctive Feature**: Golden crown of feathers
  - **Symbolism**: Good fortune, longevity, wealth (especially in Baganda culture)

### The Crested Crane Details
**Scientific Name**: Balearica regulorum gibbericeps

**Physical Characteristics**:
- Large wading bird, ~1 meter tall
- Predominantly grey body
- **Distinctive Golden Crown**: Tuft of long feathers on head (key identifier)
- **Face Markings**: Black and white patterns
- **Graceful Posture**: Upright, elegant stance

**Why This Bird?**
- ✓ Native to Uganda
- ✓ Appears on country's coat of arms
- ✓ Symbol of African grace and dignity
- ✓ Important in Baganda cultural traditions

### Technical Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio**: 2:3 (Width : Height)
- **Example**: 90cm × 135cm is correct proportion
- **Crane Position**: Centered in white disc
- **Disc Proportion**: Approximately 1/3 of flag height
- **Border**: Thin black line around white disc for definition

### Legal Protection
- Commercial use requires Minister of Justice permission
- Penalties for misuse: Up to 2 years imprisonment
- Cannot be sold for commercial purposes without license
- Disrespect is a criminal offense in Uganda
